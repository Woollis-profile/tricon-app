FAQ — Purchases

How do I restore my purchase?

If you've already purchased TriCon Training and need to restore access — for example after reinstalling the app or switching devices — open the app and go to Settings → Restore Purchase. Tap the button and your purchase will be restored automatically via the App Store.

If the restore does not work, make sure you are signed in to the same Apple ID that was used for the original purchase, then try again. If you continue to have issues, email us at support@tricontraining.app.

FAQ — Account

How do I delete my account?

You can delete your account directly inside the app. Go to Settings → Delete Account and confirm the prompt. This will permanently delete your account and all associated workout data from our servers. This action cannot be undone.

If you are unable to access the app, email support@tricontraining.app with the subject line Account Deletion Request and we will manually remove your data within 30 days.

FAQ — General

How do I contact support?

Email support@tricontraining.app with a description of your issue and your device model and iOS version if relevant. We aim to respond within 1–2 business days.
